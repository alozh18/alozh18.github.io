The only reason I still go to cinemas is so that I can eat popcorn. It is the best snack anyone has invented, especially if it's freshly made caramel popcorn.

Where best to eat popcorn:

Classic: Cinemas
Best thing about cinemas being dark is that I can munch away as much as I like.
Cons: I finish a moderately expensive box 10-20 minutes into the movie and I only know what the characters are saying through the subtitles.
Nicest and most comfortable with an added benefit of being warm: At home with family
The only time popcorn becomes the second best thing here, because the movie I watch or the snacks I eat isn't as important as the time I spend with the people I love most.

If you've never made popcorn before, here's how I'd preferably make them:

  1. Eat it
  2. Microwave it
  3. Get a microwave popcorn because a popcorn machine is still in my Amazon wishlist
